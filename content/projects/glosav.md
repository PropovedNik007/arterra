# GLOSAV

As a Machine Learning Analyst at GLOSAV from February to July 2021, I developed
a computer-vision pipeline for railway-route and obstacle detection. The work
combined object detection, tracking, temporal smoothing, and calibration for
stable results on real-time video.

I optimized the frame-processing and inference pipeline to achieve sub-5ms
latency on real-time streams.

## Contribution

The detection and tracking pipeline, performance optimization, and stability
improvements were my work.

## Stack

Python, YOLO, OpenCV, NumPy
