# susteam

From January 2023 to February 2025, I served as CTO and Data Scientist for an
ESG analytics platform for the hospitality industry. I architected and built
the product end-to-end, from document ingestion and analysis to the client
application and deployment infrastructure.

The system combined OCR with retrieval-augmented language models to automate
sustainability reporting and support explainable greenwashing analysis. I
developed a React frontend and scalable AWS services, improving data-processing
throughput by 60%.

## Contribution

Product architecture, full-stack implementation, machine-learning pipeline,
cloud infrastructure, and technical leadership.

## Stack

React, FastAPI, AWS, OCR, LangChain, RAG, BERT, LLaMA-3
