Rail4Future

I worked as a Research Scientist at TU Wien from February to July 2024, analyzing rail irregularities for the Rail4Future digital-twin platform with industrial partners Siemens and ÖBB.

Project website ↗(opens in a new tab) rail4future.com

I refactored and optimized the LSTM modeling pipeline, improving prediction accuracy by 27% while reducing training time by 40%. I also implemented statistical validation to evaluate robustness and make experiments easier to repeat.

Contribution

Data analysis, model optimization, statistical validation, and codebase modularization.

Stack

Python, TensorFlow, LSTM, statistical analysis, digital twins
