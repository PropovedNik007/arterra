About Artur

Who is Artur Sogomonyan?

Artur Sogomonyan is an AI Engineer based in Vienna, Austria. His background spans applied machine learning, computer vision, 3D geometry and toolpath software for multi-axis manufacturing, full-stack development, and hands-on making.

What does Artur Sogomonyan work on?

Artur Sogomonyan works where models meet products: shaping the data, testing the method, building the service, and making the result understandable to the person using it. His work covers applied machine learning, computer vision, 3D geometry and toolpath software for multi-axis manufacturing, LLM and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems, and full-stack web development.

What is Artur Sogomonyan’s education?

Artur Sogomonyan’s education includes an MSc Data Science at Technische Universität Wien (TU Wien, 2022–2026) and a BSc Information Technologies and Telecommunications at Bauman Moscow State Technical University (2014–2020), where his thesis was a 3D-printer failure-detection system based on computer vision. He also attended Deep Learning School (MIPT, 2020–2021) and School of Data Analysis (HSE & Yandex, 2020).

Work and skills

What is Artur Sogomonyan’s work experience?

Artur Sogomonyan currently works at Flexam on software for industrial multi-axis additive manufacturing: 3D segmentation, slicing, toolpath and G-code generation for robot arms and multi-axis machines, and the full-stack slicer product in Go, Rust, Python, and TypeScript. Artur Sogomonyan’s earlier roles include CTO & Data Scientist at susteam, Jan 2023 – Feb 2025; Research Scientist at TU Wien (Rail4Future), Feb – Jul 2024; and Machine Learning Analyst at GLOSAV, Feb – Jul 2021. Before that, he worked on 3D modelling and prototyping as well as frontend and full-stack web development.
