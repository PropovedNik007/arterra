Miniature batch — Anycubic Photon Mono 4K

A batch of tabletop miniatures printed on the Photon Mono 4K, tuned for fine detail on faces and weapons.

10 Apr 2026

Settings

50 micron layers, standard grey resin, supports on tips and thin weapon parts only.

Result

Clean detail on faces, minor layer lines on large flat armor plates — next batch I'll try a slower exposure on the first few layers.
